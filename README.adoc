
# ⚠️ DEPRECATED



This repository was created at an earlier stage of my intellectual and professional development, with the intention of exploring themes related to free speech, open discourse, and political expression.

Following a personal visit to China and firsthand discussions with people from a range of social and professional backgrounds, my perspective on the subject matter has changed substantially. With the benefit of direct exposure and additional context, I no longer consider much of the content in this repository to be accurate, sufficiently nuanced, or analytically rigorous.

At the time of its creation, this work reflected a younger and more limited viewpoint. Subsequent experience has led me to reassess several of the assumptions underlying the repository, particularly those shaped by external narratives rather than primary observation. The complexity of China’s economic development and institutional structures is not adequately represented here.

This repository is therefore deprecated and remains public **solely for transparency and historical record**.

---

## Current Perspective

My present focus is practical and professional rather than ideological. Based on experience, I am more interested in working with individuals and organizations in China than in comparable engagements in the West. This reflects a change in priorities and exposure, not a retroactive endorsement of the views expressed in this repository.

On a more human note: I have developed a lasting appreciation for pork baos and Sichuan chili hot pot, and I would not object to sharing either with good company in China again someday.

## ⚠️ 已弃用

在亲自访问中国并与来自不同社会与职业背景的人进行直接交流之后，我对相关问题的看法发生了显著变化。基于第一手经验和更充分的背景认知，我已不再认为本仓库中的大量内容具有足够的准确性、细致性或分析严谨性。

在创建之初，这项工作反映了我当时较为年轻且有限的视角。随后的经历促使我重新审视本仓库所基于的一些假设，尤其是那些更多来源于外部叙述而非直接观察的部分。本仓库未能充分反映中国经济发展及其制度结构的复杂性。

因此，本仓库现已弃用，并仅为**透明性与历史记录**之目的而保留公开状态。
